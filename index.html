About the Founder

GV Trading Platform was founded by Gyane Baraka with the vision of creating a simple, modern place where aspiring traders can understand crypto markets before taking unnecessary financial risks.

The platform combines market information, educational guides and simulated trading tools so users can focus on learning how markets work, developing discipline and understanding risk.

Our principle: education first, responsible decision-making always.

Trading Guides & Market Info

Start with the fundamentals before risking capital.

01. Understand Crypto

Learn what cryptocurrencies are, how blockchain networks work and why crypto prices can move quickly.

02. Risk Management

Never risk money you cannot afford to lose. Learn position sizing, stop-loss concepts and portfolio exposure.

03. Technical Analysis

Study price action, trends, support and resistance, volume and common chart patterns.

04. Market Psychology

Fear, greed and impulsive decisions can influence traders. Build a consistent plan and journal decisions.

05. Security

Protect accounts with strong passwords, multi-factor authentication and careful wallet practices.

06. Research First

Verify information, understand an asset's fundamentals and avoid decisions based solely on social-media hype.
