How Demo Trading Works

  1. Select an asset such as BTC/USD or ETH/USD.
  2. Choose an order style and demo amount.
  3. Select leverage to see how exposure changes.
  4. Press BUY or SELL to create a simulated position.

Risk Checklist

  • Keep position sizes controlled.
  • Use a stop-loss concept before entering.
  • Do not assume a demo result represents future performance.
  • Leverage increases both potential gains and losses.

GV Trading Guides

Learn the basics of markets, demo trading, technical analysis and risk management.

01 · Market OrdersA market order is designed to execute at the available demo price. Learn how execution price can differ as markets move.
02 · Limit OrdersA limit order specifies a desired price level. In this demo, use it to understand how conditional execution works.
03 · Stop OrdersA stop order can be used as part of an exit or entry plan when price reaches a chosen level.
04 · Position SizingDecide how much capital you are willing to expose before selecting leverage.
05 · Support & ResistanceSupport and resistance are commonly used price zones for understanding where buying or selling interest may appear.
06 · AI SignalsA signal is an analytical output, not a guarantee. Review the underlying conditions before making a decision.

Founder · GV Trading Platform

Gyane Baraka, born in 1994, is the founder of GV Trading Platform. He is a citizen of both Somalia and Kenya. The platform was created as a learning-focused digital environment for people interested in crypto markets, market analysis and responsible decision-making.

GV Trading Platform brings together educational guides, simulated trading, market dashboards and AI-style analysis in one place. The goal of the demo environment is to make complex trading concepts easier to explore without presenting simulated activity as guaranteed financial results.

Platform Vision

Build an accessible place where beginners can learn market concepts, test ideas and understand the importance of risk management.
