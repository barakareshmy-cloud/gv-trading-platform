Trading Guides

Simple learning material for people getting familiar with trading interfaces and market concepts.

1. Market OrdersA market order is designed to execute at the current available price in a trading system.
2. Limit OrdersA limit order sets a price condition rather than asking for immediate execution.
3. Support & ResistanceThese are price areas traders often watch for repeated reactions.
4. Position SizeUse a consistent risk budget and understand the amount exposed before opening a position.
5. Reading MomentumMomentum describes how quickly price is changing over a chosen observation period.
6. Demo FirstPractice with virtual funds before making decisions involving real money.
